# Music Folder

Place your MP3 file here and rename it to:

    song.mp3

Recommended songs (Westlife):
- "You Raise Me Up" — Westlife
- "Flying Without Wings" — Westlife
- "Swear It Again" — Westlife
- "My Love" — Westlife

You can download an MP3 legally from:
- YouTube Music → download for offline (if subscribed)
- Amazon Music
- Or use any personal copy you own

The site will work perfectly without the song too — the music player
simply won't play audio until the file is present.
